How to Play

  1. Move your mouse (or drag your finger) to aim the cannon.
  2. Click, tap, or press Space to shoot.
  3. Match at least three bubbles of the same color.
  4. Larger matches earn more points.
  5. Detached bubbles also fall and give bonus points.
  6. Clear the entire board to win.
  7. Don’t let bubbles reach the danger line.

Keys: ← → fine-aim · Space shoot · P pause
